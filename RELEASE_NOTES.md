# 0.7.11

Dragging the expanded player sideways now follows your finger further and arms sooner. Past the point where it would skip, it bounces, gives a light tick and an arrow slides in from the other side; letting go throws both away and brings the next artwork in. The Clock and Status bubbles no longer move or vanish under the player: their characters and icons turn out one by one while it is open and pop back in when it closes.
