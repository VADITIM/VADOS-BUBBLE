# 0.7.10

The expanded player closes on a tap, on a tap anywhere beside it and on a swipe up. Swipe right for the next song, left for the previous one, and down to pause or play. Swiping no longer crashes the app. Opening the Dashboard pops the clock's seconds and date in character by character, the settings and power icons each pop from their own spot, and the storage and RAM rows rise in with their lines filling.
