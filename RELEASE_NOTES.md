# 0.7.42

GPS in the Dashboard now shares the airplane's colour. With nothing playing, the main pill shrinks into the punch hole while the Dashboard is open. The media volume slider during a call can be dragged properly again. The clock's date no longer shoves the time when the Dashboard opens. A new setting, Clock and status pills, turns their background off; their text and icons then turn black or white to suit what is behind them. When the main pill opens, the clock and status get out of the way faster and for every opened state, and Alerts now grow out of the bar like an opened mod.
